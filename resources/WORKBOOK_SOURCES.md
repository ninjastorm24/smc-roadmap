- BabyPips and CME returned 403 to the automated request, but their pages were readable with the web tool. Both CRT pages were also readable with the web tool after automated-access errors.
- The original CFTC link concerns social-media trading hype, especially precious metals; use it for evaluating claims rather than as a general SMC lesson.
