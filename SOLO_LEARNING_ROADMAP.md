# Your eight-week SMC learning roadmap

[Start here](README.md) · [Lessons](lessons/README.md) · [Progress tracker](PROGRESS.md) · [Resources](resources/README.md)

## What you are building

A consistent chart rulebook, an annotated example library, a daily-bias worksheet, an entry-model catalog and one candidate playbook for historical and forward simulation. The DOCX supplies the lesson sequence, steps, drills and core checkpoints. The day-by-day suggestions, extra readings and templates are organizational additions.

## Pace and daily routine

The DOCX suggests **8–11 hours weekly**, six study days and one rest day. Its original class runtimes total **44h 08m 17s**; these describe the locked course videos, not the public links you need to watch.

| Daily task | Workbook budget | Output |
|---|---:|---|
| Read / watch | 30–45 min | A short concept note |
| Mark historical charts | 35–50 min | Examples labeled using available information |
| Review | 15 min | Valid, failed and ambiguous screenshots |
| Recall | 10 min | Mastery answer without notes |

This is **90–120 minutes per study day** (9–12 hours over six days), slightly above the headline budget. Lesson sheets also suggest 45–75 minutes of learning plus 45–60 minutes of chart work. Treat all these as estimates: finish the evidence before progressing. At one hour a day, split a lesson across days and stretch the curriculum to 12–16 weeks or longer.

**Evidence rule:** the weekly table below preserves the DOCX’s summary targets. The detailed chart labs on each lesson page remain the full assignment. One chart may support more than one lesson if you save the separate annotations, but never count a smaller weekly summary as permission to omit lesson drills. These are practice counts, not statistically sufficient trading samples.

## Week 1 — Foundations and swing discipline

Create a stable workspace, mark confirmed swings, and distinguish a pullback from a reversal hypothesis.

**Lesson order**

1. [01 — Orientation](lessons/01-orientation.md)
2. [02 — Pullback Identification](lessons/02-pullback-identification.md)
3. [03 — Swing Identification](lessons/03-swing-identification.md)
4. [04 — Inducement (IDM)](lessons/04-inducement.md)

**Suggested six-day plan:** Study lessons 01–04 across days 1–4. Use day 5 to revisit uncertain swings and day 6 to complete your evidence and recall checks.

**Reading:** Start with the first resource on each lesson sheet. Add [Grimes on pullbacks](resources/README.md#n05) if you need a simpler visual explanation. Read [position sizing](resources/README.md#n03) before any simulated order.

**Weekly evidence:** 30 structure/IDM examples; journal and chart workspace. Complete the full chart labs on the lesson pages.

**Move on when:** Explain your pivot rule and its confirmation delay. Show why one higher low qualifies as IDM under your rule and another does not.

## After week 8 — evaluation and four-week paper phase

The week-8 set is a first workflow test. Appendix C asks for **50–100 historical setups where available**, a later evaluation period, and a separate four-week forward phase. Extend the calendar for those samples; do not cram them into week 8 or hand-pick charts to hit a quota. Follow [the full protocol](practice/TESTING.md).

| Phase | Work | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Historical development | Finish the first sample; revise only between versions | Opportunity log and rule-change record |
| Reserved evaluation | Apply frozen rules to a later, previously unseen period | Separate report; no tuning against its outcomes |
| Paper week A | 10–15 simulated observations if available; check consistency | Decision timestamps and marking errors |
| Paper week B | Run the unchanged playbook and capture fills | Entry, exit and actual simulated fill details |
| Paper week C | Continue without midweek rule edits | Missed signals, skipped sessions and overrides |
| Paper week D | Review net R, drawdown, adherence and mistakes | Continue / refine / archive decision |

## Completion standard

- [ ] I mark swings, ranges and zones with repeatable rules.
- [ ] I record when information becomes available, not just where it appears on a finished chart.
- [ ] I can state bias, contradictory evidence and invalidation before a session.
- [ ] I have one fully defined playbook, with undefined variants kept in the catalog.
- [ ] My opportunity log includes failures, missed fills and no-entry sessions.
- [ ] My historical, evaluation and forward samples are separate.
- [ ] I can explain what the evidence supports and what remains uncertain.
