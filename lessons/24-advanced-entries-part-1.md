## Outcome

Catalog straightforward structure, POI and liquidity entries before exploring variants.

## Study in order

1. Start the entry-model library with three families: structure-based entry, POI-based entry and liquidity-sweep/reclaim entry.
2. Add common variants from the screenshot: Silver Bullet, SMT fill, accumulation-block and PSB labels, noting which terms are source-specific.
3. For each family, define a directional filter, setup sequence, trigger bar, invalidation, target and expiry condition.
4. Choose only one family for a baseline backtest. Others remain observation categories, not simultaneous signals.
5. Avoid comparing a one-minute scalp with an hourly swing setup without normalizing holding time, costs and session.
6. Use "not mapped to a public definition" rather than making up course-specific entry rules.
