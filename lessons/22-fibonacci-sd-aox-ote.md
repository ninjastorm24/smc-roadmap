## Outcome

Use one consistent swing anchor for retracements and projections without claiming universal magic levels.

## Study in order

1. Review Fibonacci retracement drawing from a defined swing low to swing high (and reverse for bearish ranges).
2. Mark equilibrium (50%) and the commonly taught OTE retracement band of roughly 62%-79% on historical examples.
3. Compare retracement zones with the broader premium/discount halves of the dealing range.
4. Study standard-deviation price projections as one possible reading of "SD", but do not assume that this is the instructor's expansion.
5. Record "AO-X" as undefined/course-specific until its level rules and anchor are verified. Do not invent a tool or method.
6. Audit outcome sensitivity: moving the anchor swing changes every retracement ratio, so lock the anchor selection rule before backtesting.

## Mastery check

Which two points anchor your Fibonacci tool, and why were they known at that time?

**Watch for:** OTE levels are descriptive price zones; they do not independently predict reversals.
