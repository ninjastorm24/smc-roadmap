## Outcome

Rank short-term and intermediate-term pivots and understand their nesting.

## Study in order

1. Use a three-candle pivot to mark short-term highs (STH) and short-term lows (STL). The right-side candle must be complete.
2. Grade an intermediate-term high (ITH) when a short-term high exceeds the adjacent short-term highs; reverse for ITL.
3. Extend the hierarchy to long-term highs/lows (LTH/LTL) as context, even though the lesson title stops at intermediate-term.
4. Show how high/low hierarchy relates to external liquidity and structural targets, while avoiding automatic importance claims.
5. Compare one 1H chart with its 15m chart. Explain why a 15m ITH need not be a 1H ITH.
6. Build a consistent labeling/color scheme for all six swing ranks and preserve the scheme across future sessions.

## Mastery check

Why can the latest swing not be classified as a completed three-candle pivot immediately?

**Watch for:** Never confuse "intermediate-term" with a fixed candle count or a particular timeframe.
