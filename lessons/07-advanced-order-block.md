# Lesson 07 — Advanced Order Block

[Roadmap](../SOLO_LEARNING_ROADMAP.md#week-4) · [All lessons](README.md) · [Progress](../PROGRESS.md)

**Week 4 · Original class runtime: 1h 16m 03s**

**Source:** Adapted directly from the DOCX class sheet. Its six study steps, chart lab and mastery check are preserved below. Public links teach related concepts; they do not reproduce a private class.

**Study budget:** 45–75 minutes learning + 45–60 minutes chart lab, plus review. Extend the week when the evidence takes longer.

## Outcome

Build reproducible OB selection and grading rules, including failed candidates.

## Study in order

1. Restate the basic bullish/bearish OB definition, then specify what counts as meaningful displacement or structural confirmation.
2. Separate a fresh OB from a previously mitigated or deeply retested zone; write a consistent rule for first and repeated touches.
3. Compare full-wick, full-body and midpoint refinements. Never change the boundary method midway through a backtest.
4. Grade OB context: external bias, liquidity event, position in premium/discount and proximity to opposing liquidity.
5. Observe whether a revisit gives clean rejection, partial mitigation, immediate invalidation or no revisit at all.
6. Create an OB quality rubric with three mandatory factors and three optional factors; record which ones actually improve results.

## Chart lab

- [ ] Collect 20 OBs: 10 that reacted and 10 that failed or were never revisited.
- [ ] Mark exact zone boundaries and one objective invalidation per example.
- [ ] Compare the same OB on 1H versus 5m before drawing conclusions.

## Mastery check

How would you distinguish an OB from any arbitrary last bearish/bullish candle?

**Watch for:** Avoid calling only successful hindsight levels "valid" after the fact.
