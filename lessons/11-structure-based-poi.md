**Companion article:** [Choosing a point of interest: make the rejection decision explicit](../blog/11-structure-based-poi.md)

## Outcome

Select candidate reaction zones using the structural story rather than arbitrary rectangles.

## Study in order

1. First identify the current external range, structural direction, recent break and nearby opposing liquidity.
2. Only then locate potential POIs: OB, FVG, breaker, prior swing, supply/demand zone or balanced price range.
3. Score each candidate by structure alignment, clarity of origin, displacement, untouched status and distance to the objective.
4. Distinguish "zone touched" from "entry signaled". Require a separate trigger if your model needs confirmation.
5. Record whether the POI is above/below equilibrium in the current dealing range, and whether another higher-timeframe zone conflicts.
6. Use a pass/no-pass rubric to reduce choice overload when three zones appear near one another.

## Chart lab

- [ ] For 15 historical swings, rank no more than two POIs before viewing the outcome.
- [ ] Record the chosen zone and one deliberately rejected zone with reasons.
- [ ] Compare the hit rate and quality of first versus repeated revisits.

## Mastery check

Explain why a zone is contextually relevant before describing the candle that formed it.

**Watch for:** A POI is a watchlist area, not a guaranteed reversal location.
