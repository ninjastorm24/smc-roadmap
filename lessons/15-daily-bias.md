**Source:** Adapted directly from the DOCX class sheet. Its six study steps, chart lab and mastery check are preserved below. Public links teach related concepts; they do not reproduce a private class.

**Study budget:** 45–75 minutes learning + 45–60 minutes chart lab, plus review. Extend the week when the evidence takes longer.

## Outcome

Write a directional scenario before a session and record when it becomes invalid.

## Study in order

1. Start with daily/4H highs and lows, recent closes, and larger visible liquidity references.
2. Identify the most plausible near-term liquidity objective, then state bullish, bearish or neutral - a neutral day is allowed.
3. Locate current price relative to the relevant dealing range, previous-day high/low and opening levels.
4. Write exactly which price condition would change the bias rather than defending one-sided predictions all day.
5. Compare a trend-continuation day, reversal day and consolidation day; avoid assuming every day offers an obvious direction.
6. Record the bias before viewing the later session, then review outcome and uncertainty without altering the original note.

## Chart lab

- [ ] Prepare pre-session daily-bias notes for 10 historical sessions.
- [ ] For each note record direction, liquidity target, invalidation and confidence (low/medium/high).
- [ ] Score direction and target independently at the end of each day.

## Mastery check

What evidence would make you mark the day as neutral or invalidate a bullish scenario?

**Watch for:** Daily bias is a testable forecast, not proof that the market must reach a stated liquidity pool.
