## Outcome

Compare confirmation and timing-oriented entry families while controlling rule complexity.

## Study in order

1. Create catalog cards for engulfing entries, MCS/WCS/DCS/SCS/MSCS, back-to-back key-level entries and OC/BPR entries.
2. Add session + CiC + QT, iFVG/rejection entries and advanced killzone entries as separate hypotheses.
3. Mark which components have independently accessible public education and which require the paid instructor's precise definition.
4. Avoid expanding ambiguous abbreviations (CiC, MCS, BPR variants) unless you can cite the original source's wording.
5. For each candidate, grade observability: can you know the setup in real time, and can two people agree on the trigger?
6. Reduce the shortlist to at most two models. Prefer fewer variables and the clearest invalidation.

## Chart lab

- [ ] Build eight entry-model cards and tag ambiguous rules.
- [ ] Use 10 blind historical examples to compare rule consistency across two models.
- [ ] Discard any model that needs retrospective adjustment to call a signal valid.

## Mastery check

Which specific entry-model labels cannot be implemented from public sources without guessing?

**Watch for:** A model with many exceptions may fit past charts while failing in forward simulation.
