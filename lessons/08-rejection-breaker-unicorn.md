# Lesson 08 — Rejection Block, Breaker Block and Unicorn Model

**Week 4 · Original class runtime: 1h 06m 46s**

**Source:** Adapted directly from the DOCX class sheet. Its six study steps, chart lab and mastery check are preserved below. Public links teach related concepts; they do not reproduce a private class.

**Study budget:** 45–75 minutes learning + 45–60 minutes chart lab, plus review. Extend the week when the evidence takes longer.

## Outcome

Differentiate a wick-based rejection area, a failed OB acting as breaker, and a breaker-FVG overlap.

## Study in order

1. Study rejection blocks as a wick-centered candidate zone; record the source's exact rules because naming and boundaries vary.
2. Identify a previously qualified OB that has been violated and later tested from the other side: a breaker-block hypothesis.
3. Map the directional structure shift or liquidity event that gives the breaker a plausible context.
4. Find the overlap between a breaker block and an FVG. That confluence is often taught as a Unicorn model.
5. Document one entry trigger, invalidation rule and target for each pattern; do not trade merely because two boxes overlap.
6. Compare positive and negative samples to test whether confluence filters reduce false signals.

## Chart lab

- [ ] Explain five cases where a claimed Unicorn overlap never produced a controlled entry.

## Mastery check

What must happen before an ordinary OB becomes a breaker in your rules?

**Watch for:** The rejection-block name and some Unicorn refinements are educator-dependent.
