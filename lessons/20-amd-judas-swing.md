## Outcome

Study accumulation/manipulation/distribution as a scenario and a timed false-open reversal hypothesis.

## Study in order

1. Read the Power of Three (PO3/AMD) narrative: initial range, excursion beyond reference levels, then directional expansion.
2. Define accumulation by observable range properties rather than after-the-fact claims about market participant intentions.
3. Mark Asian session range and a daily opening price; understand that exact session conventions depend on the market.
4. Study a Judas Swing as an early-session move through a visible level that subsequently reverses, not just any losing breakout.
5. Write rules for both a successful reversal and the alternate case where price keeps expanding in the original direction.
6. Use actual historical days to test whether the AMD classification was possible before the later expansion.

## Chart lab

- [ ] Track 12 historical days, classifying range, sweep, continuation and non-fitting days.
- [ ] For eight suspected Judas Swings, mark the opening price, swept level and recovery close.
- [ ] Count the days that never formed a clean AMD narrative.

## Mastery check

What observable event prevents you from calling a breakout a confirmed Judas Swing prematurely?

**Watch for:** AMD is an interpretive framework, not proof of manipulation by identified institutions.
