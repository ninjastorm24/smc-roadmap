## Outcome

Distinguish two documented confirmation ideas and formally define the course-specific labels.

## Study in order

1. Review market structure shift (MSS): a relevant opposing structural break, often associated with displacement.
2. Study change in state of delivery (CISD) using the chosen free source; note that it may use candle sequences rather than the same pivot as MSS.
3. Annotate how MSS and CISD can occur in different candles or disagree on timing within the same reversal.
4. Treat TBS and TWS as instructor-dependent abbreviations. Do not invent their full forms or entry rules without the original explanation.
5. Create a provisional glossary: for each undefined term, record exact trigger candle, required context and failure condition once verified.
6. Compare a confirmed shift, an unconfirmed stop-run and a continuation on at least one timeframe.
