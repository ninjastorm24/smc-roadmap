## Outcome

Differentiate a proposed inducement swing from a normal HL/LH using explicit criteria.

## Study in order

1. Write the IDM definition used by your chosen SMC source. Teachers disagree on whether inducement is a minor swing, a liquidity lure or a mandatory pre-BOS level.
2. Draw the current external high/low and the smaller internal swings leading into a prospective larger objective.
3. Mark which small lows/highs could plausibly contain protective stops and which levels are merely ordinary noise.
4. Compare two case types: a minor swing being swept before continuation, and a minor swing breaking without a meaningful reversal.
5. Set a rule that IDM is not a guaranteed sweep; avoid relabeling every failed setup as inducement after the move.
6. Revisit your earlier chart markings and classify each disputed IDM point as confirmed under your rule, uncertain or invalid.

## Chart lab

- [ ] Collect 20 potential IDM situations; record 10 clear and 10 ambiguous examples.
- [ ] Mark the level, the intended external target and the invalidation point.
- [ ] Write a one-sentence reason why each proposed IDM is not simply an HL/LH.

## Mastery check

When is a higher low a structural HL but not necessarily IDM? Defend the distinction in one chart.

**Watch for:** An IDM label is an interpretation of visible price action, not proof that institutions engineered a trap.
