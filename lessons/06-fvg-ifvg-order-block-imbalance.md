# Lesson 06 — FVG, iFVG, Order Block and Imbalance

[Roadmap](../SOLO_LEARNING_ROADMAP.md#week-3) · [All lessons](README.md) · [Progress](../PROGRESS.md)

**Week 3 · Original class runtime: 1h 27m 08s**

**Source:** Adapted directly from the DOCX class sheet. Its six study steps, chart lab and mastery check are preserved below. Public links teach related concepts; they do not reproduce a private class.

**Study budget:** 45–75 minutes learning + 45–60 minutes chart lab, plus review. Extend the week when the evidence takes longer.

## Outcome

Identify three-candle imbalances and compare them with order blocks and inverse FVGs.

## Study in order

1. For a bullish three-candle FVG, check that candle 3 low is above candle 1 high; reverse the inequality for bearish gaps.
2. Mark the exact gap boundaries and the midpoint (consequent encroachment). Record whether the gap appears after displacement.
3. Distinguish a simple imbalance, a traded-through or filled gap, and an inversion FVG in which the former gap loses its original role.
4. Locate the last opposing candle before a clear directional expansion as an order-block candidate. Identify its body, wick and midpoint.
5. Compare five FVGs that align with higher-timeframe direction against five that form randomly mid-range.
6. Write an invalidation and retest rule for each zone rather than assuming every FVG or OB must be respected.

## Chart lab

- [ ] Mark 20 FVGs (10 bullish/10 bearish) and note their fill/inversion outcomes.
- [ ] Mark 10 OB candidates and distinguish each from nearby FVGs.
- [ ] Save five counterexamples where an attractive zone failed.

## Mastery check

Can you identify the three candles creating an FVG without using an indicator?

**Watch for:** Not all gaps fill, and a gap or OB is not evidence of the exact underlying institutional orders.
