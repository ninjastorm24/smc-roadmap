# Lesson 17 — Applying Advanced Bias on a Chart

**Source:** Adapted directly from the DOCX class sheet. Its six study steps, chart lab and mastery check are preserved below. Public links teach related concepts; they do not reproduce a private class.

**Study budget:** 45–75 minutes learning + 45–60 minutes chart lab, plus review. Extend the week when the evidence takes longer.

## Outcome

Perform a reproducible daily-to-intraday analysis before a simulated session.

## Study in order

1. Begin with a blank daily chart; mark previous day high/low, major swing, dealing range and likely external target.
2. Write the day's bullish, bearish or neutral bias and the exact price level that would invalidate it.
3. Move to the 1H chart to identify structure, relevant POIs, recent liquidity events and current phase.
4. Move to 15m/5m only to define entry conditions. Do not let lower-timeframe noise reverse a well-defined HTF plan without a rule.
5. Define the no-trade conditions: conflicting structure, major news, insufficient target room, unclear stop or late entry.
6. Save the pre-session screenshot and compare it with the end-of-session screenshot with no retrospective redraw.

## Chart lab

- [ ] Complete 10 full top-down markups using one instrument.
- [ ] Keep two screenshots per session: before and after.
- [ ] Record whether bias was correct, whether an entry existed and whether the plan was followed.

## Mastery check

Can another trader understand your thesis, trigger and invalidation from one pre-session page?

**Watch for:** Do not revise the pre-session thesis to make hindsight appear accurate.
