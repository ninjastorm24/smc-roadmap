**Source:** Adapted directly from the DOCX class sheet. Its six study steps, chart lab and mastery check are preserved below. Public links teach related concepts; they do not reproduce a private class.

**Study budget:** 45–75 minutes learning + 45–60 minutes chart lab, plus review. Extend the week when the evidence takes longer.

## Outcome

Set up a trading-learning environment and decide exactly what will be measured before studying patterns.

## Study in order

1. Identify the market you will study (for example one forex pair, index future, or cryptocurrency). Do not switch instruments during your first four weeks.
2. Learn candlestick OHLC, chart timeframes, order types, bid/ask spread, leverage and the distinction between chart analysis and execution.
3. Open a free chart workspace and a paper-trading account. Set the chart timezone explicitly and record it in your journal.
4. Create five standard chart templates: blank candlesticks, swing structure, liquidity levels, PD arrays and top-down analysis.
5. Define a screenshot-naming convention and an evidence log. Marking a chart is practice; a rule and outcome log is testing.
6. Write your initial risk policy: simulated trading only while learning, no borrowed money, no guaranteed-income expectations.

## Chart lab

- [ ] Save a clean chart and identify open, high, low and close on five candles.
- [ ] Mark yesterday's high and low and label your chart timezone.
- [ ] Create a journal with date, symbol, timeframe, setup, bias, stop, target, fees, result in R and screenshot.

## Mastery check

Explain why a chart pattern alone does not establish an edge; demonstrate a simulated order with a stop.

**Watch for:** Avoid purchasing indicators or using real capital before you can define a setup.
