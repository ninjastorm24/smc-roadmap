## Outcome

Mark objective swing highs/lows and build a consistent HH-HL-LH-LL sequence.

## Study in order

1. Start with a mechanical three-candle pivot rule: a swing high is above adjacent candle highs; a low is below adjacent lows.
2. Confirm that a pivot requires subsequent candles. Do not label the newest unconfirmed candle as a completed swing.
3. Connect significant pivots to identify HH/HL in an uptrend, LH/LL in a downtrend and the messy overlap of ranges.
4. Distinguish local internal pivots from external pivots that define the broader swing. Use identical rules in every screenshot.
5. Mark equal highs/lows separately; decide whether equal tolerance uses tick size or a stated price buffer.
6. Compare pivot structure on 1H and 15m and show how one 1H leg may contain several 15m swings.

## Chart lab

- [ ] Label 25 swings on one 1H chart and 25 on a 15m chart.
- [ ] Circle five pivots that looked valid but had not yet been confirmed.
- [ ] Save 10 HH-HL / LH-LL examples and five ranging counterexamples.

## Mastery check

Can another student reproduce your swing marks using your written rule?

**Watch for:** Avoid choosing the most attractive swing retrospectively after knowing the result.
