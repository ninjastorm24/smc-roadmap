# Lesson 09 — Mitigation Block, Reclaimed Block and QML

**Week 4 · Original class runtime: 1h 28m 49s**

**Source:** Adapted directly from the DOCX class sheet. Its six study steps, chart lab and mastery check are preserved below. Public links teach related concepts; they do not reproduce a private class.

**Study budget:** 45–75 minutes learning + 45–60 minutes chart lab, plus review. Extend the week when the evidence takes longer.

## Outcome

Compare block re-use/reaction concepts with the Quasimodo reversal pattern.

## Study in order

1. Read one consistent mitigation-block definition and identify the swing and retracement behavior the source requires.
2. For a reclaimed OB, separate the descriptive idea (a level regained after being lost) from any instructor-specific formal rule.
3. Learn the bearish QML sequence: high, higher high, structural low break, then retest toward the left-shoulder area; mirror for bullish.
4. Explain how QML differs from a simple double top/bottom or an unconfirmed head-and-shoulders.
5. Choose a fixed zone-placement method for each pattern; explicitly flag cases in which boundaries are subjective.
6. Use only demo/historical charts until you have an independent definition that produces repeatable marks.

- [ ] Write a four-event sequence next to every QML rather than drawing only a line.

## Mastery check

Name the structural sequence that qualifies a bearish QML.

**Watch for:** Reclaimed-block definitions differ widely: treat a free lesson as a related concept, not the paid instructor's exact method.
