## Outcome

Understand how swings, ranges and structural shifts nest across timeframes.

## Study in order

1. Compare a single 4H candle to its constituent 1H and 15m candles; note that the same high/low can contain many internal events.
2. Create a top-down map: Daily = broad context, 1H = intermediate structure, 5m/15m = execution detail.
3. Identify a lower-timeframe CHoCH that did not invalidate the higher-timeframe trend.
4. Explain how opposing ranges can coexist and why a small-timeframe FVG may conflict with a large-timeframe liquidity draw.
5. Design two timeframe pairings appropriate to your market; avoid changing them after the trade works or fails.
6. Practice eliminating irrelevant lower-timeframe noise while retaining exact entry timing for your chosen model.

## Mastery check

Can you explain the same price move as a 5m reversal and a 1H pullback?

**Watch for:** Fractality describes nesting; it does not mean identical trading accuracy at every timeframe.
