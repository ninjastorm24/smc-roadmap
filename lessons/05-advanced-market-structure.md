**Source:** Adapted directly from the DOCX class sheet. Its six study steps, chart lab and mastery check are preserved below. Public links teach related concepts; they do not reproduce a private class.

**Study budget:** 45–75 minutes learning + 45–60 minutes chart lab, plus review. Extend the week when the evidence takes longer.

## Outcome

Integrate internal/external swings, BOS, CHoCH, protected highs/lows and displacement.

## Study in order

1. Map the current external range, the last confirmed external break and the internal moves contained within it.
2. Define a continuation BOS and a countertrend CHoCH/MSS. Choose whether your system requires a candle body close beyond a specific swing.
3. Identify protected versus vulnerable structural levels. Explain what price must do to invalidate the current directional read.
4. Separate a weak wick penetration from a decisive break with displacement. Record why some apparent breaks fail.
5. Walk forward candle by candle and update structure without moving yesterday's labels to fit the final outcome.
6. Write rules for conflict resolution: e.g. bearish 5m internal CHoCH inside a bullish 1H external leg.

## Chart lab

- [ ] Draw 10 full bullish and 10 full bearish structure maps with BOS/CHoCH labels.
- [ ] Write the protected level and invalidation rule on each map.
- [ ] Annotate five examples where internal and external bias disagree.

## Mastery check

Explain why a lower-timeframe CHoCH may remain just a higher-timeframe pullback.

**Watch for:** Differentiate a wick sweep from a closed structural break in your definitions.
