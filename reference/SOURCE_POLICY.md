## Editorial decisions

- Preserve each class’s title, duration, six steps, chart-lab tasks, mastery check and interpretation warning. Change formatting and add navigation and optional readings.
- Preserve the DOCX’s order: `01, 02, 03, 04 / 05, 10, 14 / 12, 13, 06 / 07, 08, 09, 11 / 15, 16, 17 / 19, 20, 22, 23 / 18, 21, 24 / 25, 26`.
- Keep the headline eight-week pace, while explicitly showing that the DOCX’s daily timing and detailed exercise quotas can require more time. Weekly summary counts do not replace class drills.
- Preserve the workbook’s 20–30 initial blind scenarios, 50–100 extended historical setups where available, later evaluation period and separate four-week paper phase. These are different stages, not competing completion thresholds.
- Clarify expectancy: subtract costs once from gross outcomes, or average already-net R. The workbook’s win/loss formula requires gross average winners/losers if costs are subtracted afterward.
- Add official platform documentation to distinguish historical Replay Trading from forward Paper Trading. Do not imply that every replay/data feature is free.
- Keep instructor-dependent terms unresolved. Public CRT and quarterly-theory material may explain related variants; it does not resolve missing TBS, TWS, AO-X or fixed-entry rules.
- Add 12 new external URLs, a curated blog list, reusable Markdown templates, and a progress tracker. These are editorial supplements.
