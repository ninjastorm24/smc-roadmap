# Advanced entries, part 1: sort the families before choosing a model

Structure, POI, liquidity, Silver Bullet, SMT Fill, Accu Block and PSB need distinct definitions.

**Lesson 24 · Week 7 · About 3 minutes**

[Series index](README.md) · [Lesson worksheet](../lessons/24-advanced-entries-part-1.md) · [Roadmap](../SOLO_LEARNING_ROADMAP.md#week-7)

A catalog of entry names can create the impression that learning more names automatically creates more opportunity. In practice, several names may describe closely related observations, while others conceal missing rules.

Part 1 of the workbook’s entry lab asks you to organize the families and choose one baseline. The selection should depend on clarity and repeatability before performance claims enter the discussion.

## Begin with the three broad families

A structure-based entry makes a defined structural event central to eligibility. A POI-based entry starts from a preselected qualifying zone. A liquidity-based entry depends on a specified interaction with a marked reference.

These families can overlap. A sweep followed by a structural break and gap revisit contains all three. Calling it three strategies would not produce three independent samples.

For each candidate, write context, location, trigger, invalidation, target and expiry. If you cannot identify the first knowable candle, the card is not ready for testing.

## Give each advertised variant a source status

| Family | What can be studied now | What still needs explicit rules |
|---|---|---|
| Silver Bullet | Public timed FVG-style explanations | Source-specific window, zone, entry and exit conditions |
| SMT Fill | Public comparisons between related instruments | The course’s exact fill trigger and variations |
| Accumulation Block / Accu Block | Its place in the course catalog | Qualifying block, boundaries and entry sequence |
| PSB | The name appears in the workbook | Acronym expansion and complete model definition |

The workbook’s [Silver Bullet article](https://ttrades.com/the-am-silver-bullet-strategy-on-nq/) describes one market-specific public version. [TTrades’ SMT comparison](https://ttrades.com/relative-strength-weakness-smt-divergence-full-guide/) supplies public divergence concepts, not the missing SMT Fill method.

## A hypothetical shortlist decision

Candidate A has an explicit premarked level, close-based confirmation and first-revisit expiry. Candidate B requires “strong confluence” and a proprietary variation whose trigger is unavailable.

For a baseline research exercise, A is implementable sooner. That does not establish that it will be profitable. It means the learner can produce an honest eligibility log and discover whether the stated rules are usable.

Keep B in the catalog as partially mapped or undefined. Skipping a model with missing rules is different from rejecting it on performance.

## Compare models on compatible terms

A one-minute entry and an hourly entry may differ in observation window, costs, holding time and opportunity count. Record those differences before comparing win rates. Use the same interpretation of costs and completed trades.

Choose one model for the first sample. Save five examples and counterexamples for each broad family to learn recognition, then move to a preselected continuous period for testing. Examples chosen because they look clear remain study material, even when they illustrate the model beautifully.
