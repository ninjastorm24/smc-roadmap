## How to read this series

Each article explains the idea in plain language, works through an example or decision process, identifies common errors and gives you a practice task. The lesson worksheet remains the source for the full assignment.

The [DOCX workbook](../SMC_Learning_Workbook.docx) defines the curriculum. These articles are original educational expansions, not transcripts of the locked lessons. Price examples are hypothetical; proposed research rules are labeled. Instructor-specific methods stay undefined where their actual rules are unavailable. External links document particular teaching conventions, not independently verified profitability.

Use the previous/next links to follow the study sequence. Class numbers remain the original numbers so you can match each article to the workbook.

## Topic coverage

- **Foundations:** candles, order types, costs, pullbacks, swings and confirmation delay.
- **Structure:** IDM, BOS, CHoCH, protected levels, short/intermediate-term pivots and timeframe nesting.
- **Zones:** FVG, iFVG, OB, rejection, breaker, Unicorn, mitigation, reclaimed blocks, QML and POI selection.
- **Liquidity and context:** external/internal references, grabs/sweeps, pools, voids, supply/demand, premium/discount and dealing ranges.
- **Planning:** daily bias, session timing, OHLC references, event context and top-down application.
- **Models:** strategy construction, MSS/CISD, AMD/Judas, CRT/TBS, Fibonacci/OTE, SD/AO-X and quarterly theory.
- **Entry catalog:** every family from workbook Appendix A appears in articles 21 and 24–26; source-specific gaps are stated explicitly.
- **Evaluation:** position sizing, execution assumptions, journaling, rule adherence, historical testing, reserved evaluation and forward simulation.

## Keep the learning record

After an article, write a [concept note](../templates/CONCEPT_NOTE.md), complete the linked worksheet and update [your progress](../PROGRESS.md). Keep failed and ambiguous examples. You do not need to adopt every model discussed in order to finish the curriculum.
